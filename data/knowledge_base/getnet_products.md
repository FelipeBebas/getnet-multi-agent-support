# Getnet Official Product & Institutional Knowledge Base

## Getnet Global & Institutional Overview
source_url: https://www.getnet.net/en
product_tag: institutional_overview

Getnet is a global merchant payment solutions and fintech company part of **PagoNxt** (the payments backbone of **Grupo Santander**). Operating across Latin America (Brazil, Mexico, Chile, Argentina, Uruguay) and Europe (Spain, Portugal), Getnet serves everything from micro-entrepreneurs (MEI/PF) to large enterprise retailers.

Core capabilities documented on `https://www.getnet.net/en` and `https://site.getnet.com.br`:
- **In-Store Acquiring (POS Terminals)**: Full lineup of smart and classic card machines (*Get Tap*, *Get Mini*, *Get Lite*, *Get Clássica*, and *Get Smart*) accepting all major credit, debit, voucher, and NFC contactless brands plus Pix QR Code.
- **Digital & Social Commerce**: E-commerce gateway, recurring billing, and **Payment Link (*GetPay*)** for selling directly on **WhatsApp** and social media without a website.
- **Financial & Working Capital Solutions**: **Receivables Advance (*Antecipação de Recebíveis*)**, **Crediário** long-term installment financing, and **GetConta** digital business account.

## POS Terminal Comparison: Get Clássica vs. Get Smart (and Full Maquininhas Lineup)
source_url: https://site.getnet.com.br/maquininhas/
product_tag: pos_terminals_comparison

### Difference Between Get Clássica and Get Smart
When comparing **Get Clássica** and **Get Smart**, both are high-performance Getnet terminals with **built-in thermal paper receipt printers (*impressora de bobina*)**, Wi-Fi + mobile network connectivity with a free SIM data plan, and NFC contactless + Chip acceptance, but they are built for different operational profiles:

1. **Get Clássica (Traditional Countertop & Fast Checkout POS)**
   - **Interface & Hardware**: Features a **physical tactile keypad** (*teclado físico*) paired with a color screen.
   - **Connectivity**: **Wi-Fi + 3G/4G SIM card** with a free multi-operator data plan.
   - **Receipt Printing**: Built-in **thermal paper roll printer (*bobina*)** plus optional SMS receipt.
   - **Payment Acceptance**: Chip & PIN, Magnetic Stripe, **NFC Contactless** (Apple Pay, Google Pay, Samsung Pay, contactless cards), Credit, Debit, Vouchers, **Pix QR Code**, and **Crediário**.
   - **Best For**: High-throughput retail counters, bakeries, pharmacies, and merchants who prefer the speed and durability of a physical keypad without needing Android business management apps on the terminal.

2. **Get Smart (Android Smart POS with Touchscreen & Apps)**
   - **Interface & Hardware**: Runs an **Android operating system** on a large **HD multi-touch screen** (100% touchscreen, no physical keypad) with an integrated **camera/barcode scanner** and long-lasting battery.
   - **Connectivity**: **Wi-Fi + 4G SIM card + Bluetooth**.
   - **Receipt Printing**: High-speed built-in **thermal paper roll printer (*bobina*)** plus digital receipt via SMS or email.
   - **Smart Ecosystem (*GetStore*)**: Allows merchants to install business management apps directly on the card machine — including product catalog, inventory control, restaurant table/order management, bill splitting, and ERP/POS integration.
   - **Best For**: Restaurants, bars, clinics, fashion retail, and merchants who want an all-in-one point-of-sale computer + payment terminal in a single Android device.

### Complete Getnet Terminal Portfolio (All 5 Models)
- **Get Tap (Tap on Phone)**: Free app that transforms an Android smartphone with NFC (Android 9+) into a Getnet card machine for contactless credit, debit, and Pix sales.
- **Get Mini**: Ultra-compact pocket terminal with physical keypad, 3G SIM + Wi-Fi, NFC contactless, and digital receipt via SMS (no paper roll). Ideal for autonomous professionals and delivery couriers.
- **Get Lite**: Compact portable terminal with physical keypad, Wi-Fi + 3G/4G chip, NFC contactless, extended battery life, and SMS digital receipts (no paper roll).
- **Get Clássica**: Robust terminal with **physical keypad** and **printed paper roll receipt (*bobina*)**, Wi-Fi + 3G/4G, and NFC contactless.
- **Get Smart**: Advanced **Android touchscreen Smart POS** with **printed paper roll receipt (*bobina*)**, Wi-Fi + 4G, camera, and **GetStore** business management apps.

## Pix Sales on Getnet & Bank Account (Domicílio Bancário) Requirements
source_url: https://site.getnet.com.br/duvidas/
product_tag: pix_bank_account_rules

### Do I Need a Bank Account to Receive Sales via Pix on Getnet?
- **Yes, you need a registered bank account (*domicílio bancário*) or a GetConta digital account** to receive your sales payouts via Pix (as well as credit and debit card settlements) with Getnet.
- **Does it have to be a Santander account? No.** Merchants can register a checking account (*conta corrente*) or savings account (*conta poupança*) from **any bank in Brazil** (Santander, Bradesco, Itaú, Banco do Brasil, Caixa, Nubank, Inter, etc.), or use Getnet's own **GetConta** digital account.
- **Ownership Rule (Same CPF or CNPJ)**: For security and Central Bank compliance, the registered bank account (*domicílio bancário*) **must belong to the same holder** as the Getnet merchant registration (CPF-to-CPF for individual merchants, or CNPJ-to-CNPJ for company accounts).
- **How Pix Works on Getnet**: Merchants can generate dynamic Pix QR Codes directly on the screen of their Getnet card machine (*Get Clássica*, *Get Smart*, *Get Lite*, *Get Mini*, *Get Tap*) or via Payment Link, with instant confirmation on the terminal and automated reconciliation in the *Minha Getnet* portal and app.

## Receivables Advance (Antecipação de Recebíveis) on Getnet
source_url: https://site.getnet.com.br/antecipacao-de-recebiveis/
product_tag: receivables_advance_antecipacao

### How Does Receivables Advance (Antecipação) Work with Getnet?
**Receivables Advance (*Antecipação de Recebíveis*)** is a Getnet financial service that allows merchants to bring forward the payout of their credit card sales (both single-payment *crédito à vista* and installment sales *crédito parcelado em até 12x*), receiving the money in **1 business day (D+1)**, **2 business days (D+2)**, or even on the **same day (D+0)** instead of waiting 30 to 360 days.

### Modalities of Antecipação with Getnet
1. **Automatic Advance (*Antecipação Automática / Plano Receba Já*)**:
   - Once activated, every credit card sale made on the POS terminal or online is automatically advanced and deposited into the merchant's registered bank account according to the contracted schedule (such as D+1 or D+2).
2. **Punctual / On-Demand Advance (*Antecipação Avulsa ou Pontual*)**:
   - The merchant chooses when and how much of their existing future receivables schedule they want to advance.
   - Requests can be simulated and contracted in seconds through the **App Getnet**, **Portal Minha Getnet**, **Getnet WhatsApp**, or the Customer Support Center.

### Key Benefits & Rules
- **Cash Flow Predictability**: Immediate working capital to pay suppliers, restock inventory, or cover operational expenses without taking out a traditional bank loan (no IOF tax on standard receivables advance).
- **Transparent Pricing**: A proportional discount fee (*taxa de antecipação*) is applied based on the term of the installments being brought forward; the exact net deposit amount is shown before confirmation.
- **Cutoff Times**: Punctual requests confirmed on business days before the afternoon cutoff window are settled on the same day or next business day (D+1) into the merchant's registered *domicílio bancário*.

## Crediário on Getnet: Maximum Installments & Merchant Payout
source_url: https://site.getnet.com.br/duvidas/crediario/
product_tag: crediario_installments

### How Many Installments Can I Split a Sale Into with the Crediário?
- With Getnet **Crediário** on the card machine, a merchant can split a sale into **up to 48 installments (up to 48x)** (or up to 24x / 36x / 48x depending on the cardholder's issuing bank rules, such as Santander and partner banks).
- Standard credit card installment sales (*crédito parcelado lojista*) normally allow up to 12 installments, whereas **Crediário Getnet expands installment capacity up to 48x** using the customer's pre-approved bank financing limit.

### How Crediário Works for the Merchant and Customer
1. **Simulation on the Terminal**: At checkout on *Get Clássica*, *Get Smart*, or *Get Lite*, the merchant selects the **Crediário** option, enters the sale amount, and simulates the number of installments (up to **48x**) and exact monthly installment values directly on the machine screen before the customer enters their PIN.
2. **Upfront Merchant Settlement (D+2)**: Even when the customer chooses to pay in up to **48 installments**, the **merchant receives the net sale value upfront in 2 business days (D+2)** (or according to their contracted settlement plan), with **zero default risk** for the store (the card-issuing bank assumes the credit risk).
3. **Ideal Use Cases**: High-ticket purchases such as electronics, furniture, home appliances, auto parts, dental/medical treatments, construction materials, and travel packages.

## Payment Link (Link de Pagamento / GetPay) & Selling via WhatsApp
source_url: https://site.getnet.com.br/link-de-pagamento/
product_tag: payment_link_whatsapp

### Can I Sell Through WhatsApp Using the Getnet Payment Link?
- **Yes! You can sell through WhatsApp using the Getnet Payment Link (*GetPay*)** quickly and securely, without needing an e-commerce website or a physical card machine (with **Credit Card in up to 12x**, Debit, Pix, Boleto, and integrated **Anti-Fraud** protection).
- In addition to **WhatsApp**, the Payment Link can be shared with customers on **Instagram Direct**, **Facebook Messenger**, **Telegram**, **SMS**, or **Email**.

### Step-by-Step: How to Sell on WhatsApp with GetPay Payment Link
1. **Generate the Link**: Open the **App Getnet** or **Portal Minha Getnet** and access **Link de Pagamento (GetPay)**.
2. **Configure the Sale**: Enter the product/service name, sale value, and choose the payment methods you want to offer the customer:
   - **Credit Card** (single payment *à vista* or split in **up to 12 installments / 12x**).
   - **Debit Card** and **Digital Wallets**.
   - **Pix** and **Boleto Bancário**.
3. **Share on WhatsApp**: Copy the generated URL and send it directly in the **WhatsApp** conversation with your customer.
4. **Secure Checkout & Anti-Fraud**: The customer clicks the link, opens a secure Getnet checkout page in their browser, and completes payment protected by Getnet's **integrated Anti-Fraud system** and **3DS 2.0 authentication**.
5. **Tracking & Settlement**: The merchant tracks payment status (Paid, Pending, Expired) in real time inside the App Getnet and receives the funds in their registered bank account according to their contracted settlement or *Antecipação* plan.
